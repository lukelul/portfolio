About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Texas at Austin, currently building Inhabit — my own robotics teleoperation hardware company, backed by Founders Inc. and NVIDIA Inception — alongside robotics research through the UT Center for Autonomy.

For the full project history and work experience, see Experience and CAD above.

Previously, I built the forearm and hand linkage for a humanoid arm at YouiBot in Shenzhen. I also led a VEX Robotics team to the 2024 World Championship, and picked up hardware wins at the UC Berkeley AI Hackathon and the Texas Venture Group Hackathon.

Robotics is the next platform shift, and whoever makes hardware and data collection radically cheaper wins it.

Along the way I've designed 20+ robots and built a robotics education community of 14,000+ members. (I'm a native speaker of English and Mandarin.)
